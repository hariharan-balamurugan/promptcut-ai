# 🎬 PromptCut AI

**AI-Powered Video Editing Using Natural Language Processing**

PromptCut AI is an intelligent video editing application that enables users to edit videos using simple natural-language instructions. Instead of manually applying effects and editing every scene, users can describe their desired edits, and the AI system interprets the instructions and automatically performs the editing operations.

The application combines Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, computer vision, and audio processing to create an automated video editing experience.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI Prompt Understanding:** Converts natural-language editing instructions into structured editing operations.
- **Video Analysis:** Extracts and analyzes video frames to identify scene characteristics and emotions.
- **Emotion-Based Music Selection:** Selects background music according to the emotional context of the video.
- **Intelligent Music Synchronization:** Matches music segments with video emotion timelines.
- **Slow Motion & Fast Motion:** Adjusts video playback speed.
- **Cinematic Effects:** Supports fade-in, fade-out, zoom-in, and zoom-out effects.
- **Directional Slide Transitions:** Supports left-to-right, right-to-left, top-to-bottom, and bottom-to-top transitions.
- **Karaoke-Style Captions:** Generates word-by-word captions from background music lyrics.
- **Automated Video Export:** Processes editing operations and exports the final video.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

**Backend:** Python, FastAPI

**Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript

**AI & NLP:** Ollama, Qwen 3.5:4B

**Video Processing:** FFmpeg, FFprobe

**Audio Processing:** Librosa, Demucs, Faster-Whisper

**Database:** SQLite

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Upload a video.
2. Enter editing instructions using natural language.
3. AI interprets the editing prompt.
4. The system analyzes video frames and emotional characteristics.
5. Suitable background music is selected from the music catalog.
6. Music segments are matched with the video timeline.
7. Requested effects and transitions are applied.
8. Karaoke captions are generated from background music lyrics when requested.
9. The final edited video is exported.
